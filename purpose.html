Website & Project Purpose

This website is committed to popularizing professional rehabilitation knowledge, especially focusing on single-mode assisted walking rehabilitation methods applied in rehabilitation centers for people with lower limb weakness and impaired walking ability. It provides standardized, easy-to-understand rehabilitation science guidance for patients, families and rehabilitation practitioners.

All of my projects are designed and developed for socially vulnerable groups with movement disorders. The lower limb exoskeleton targets patients with insufficient leg strength and mobility difficulties, while the hand stabilization device serves Parkinson’s patients with involuntary tremors. All creations aim to solve real-life rehabilitation pain points and provide low-cost, practical intelligent rehabilitation solutions.
